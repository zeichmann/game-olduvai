Zabol: Stone Age character mod

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool, then integrated into the HD renderer.
References: AkademiaOli/public/games/wyspy-wiedzy/assets/akademia/zabol-run-0.webp
and AkademiaOli/public/games/boulder-dash/images/island/zabol-player-v1.png.

Main prompt:
Create an eight-pose, transparent sprite sheet of the same friendly frog Zabol
from the references as a Stone Age caveman. Preserve lime-green skin, glossy
brown protruding eyes, pale-yellow muzzle, broad smile and cheerful personality.
Replace pirate clothes and hat with a tawny leopard-fur tunic, brown fur shoulder
strap and ivory bone pin, with bare green webbed feet and bare head. Smooth
polished cartoon rendering; no pixel art. Four columns, two rows: idle, three
walking poses, jump, empty-handed attack, climb, hurt/fall. All poses face right.
No text, background, scenery, shadows or weapons (the engine renders the club).

Cleanup prompt:
Preserve the eight characters, identity, costume, poses and grid. Remove colored
spray, speckles, noise and halos outside the silhouettes. Clean antialiased
transparent boundaries; preserve internal skin and fur. No redesign or labels.

Launch through the workspace's Uruchom Prehistorik.cmd. It sets
OLDUVAI_PLAYER_SHEET to stone-age-sprites.png and selects soft HD at scale 6.
Without that environment variable, the original player renderer is used.
The soft profile uses xBRZ followed by a cached premultiplied-alpha soft finish.
Physics, hitboxes and level data are unchanged.

The repository-portable equivalent is mods/zabol/launch.cmd.

Version 2: stone-age-sprites-v2.png (built-in image_gen).
Prompt: preserve Zabol's identity; add a rust-red cloth bandana in every frame
and a chunky wooden club carried in idle, running and jump poses. Matte painted
storybook shading, muted olive/ochre, soft edges, no plastic gloss. Transparent
4-column by 5-row sheet: idle/open, half-closed eyes, closed eyes, recovery;
eight consecutive natural running phases across rows 2 and 3; jump, attack,
hurt, breathing in row 4; four alternating rear-view ladder climbing phases
in row 5 with the club strapped to his back. Constant character size and foot
baseline, no ladder, no background, no captions or grid lines.
V2 uses distance-driven run/climb sampling and timed idle blinking. Runtime
desaturation and alpha-aware soft reconstruction match the character to the
environment. Original V1 sheet is retained as a reference.

Revision 3: the launchers now use xBRZ without the soft/blur profile.
The character reconstruction blur has been removed. Running uses V2's stable
torso with two independently articulated legs and inverse-kinematics knees.
The 32-phase gait has alternate support feet; during stance each foot's backward
motion cancels body travel. Phase advances from authoritative physics positions,
with interpolation used only for rendering, rather than counting render passes.
The generated V2 run silhouettes are no longer used for locomotion. Bandana,
carried club, blink, jump/attack and rear-view ladder poses remain in use.

Revision 4 supersedes revision 3. The procedural lower-body/IK renderer was
removed. Ground movement now renders the complete original body sprite for
each original physics animation frame, retaining its pelvis, shoulder, arm
and leg movement and frame offsets. Its skin palette is retinted olive green.
A separate bandana frog head follows that frame's head silhouette. The club
is carried in the leading hand at 55 percent of its attack-sprite size, with
the handle attached to the palm in each body frame and the head facing outwards.
The original full-size attack frames are preserved during an actual swing.
Back-facing V2 climbing poses are retained. No image blur is applied.

stone-age-head.png was generated with built-in image_gen, referencing V2:
head-only atlas, 2 columns x 2 rows; Zabol's green skin, brown eyes, pale-yellow
muzzle and rust-red bandana. Open, half-closed and closed eyelids facing right,
plus a back view. Consistent scale, clean silhouette, crisp painted shading,
transparent background, no body/arms/weapons/text/glow/blur.
